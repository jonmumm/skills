# Playtesting with the kids

The kids are the ground truth. Critic scores are a proxy for them.

## Session rules

- Short: 10–15 minutes, at the kids' normal game time (bedtime games at bedtime).
- The grown-up plays their real role, not a tester role. Don't explain more than a real family
  would.
- Record if possible, with the kids' consent in spirit (never publish it): the TV via the game's
  recorder, and a phone video of the couch for faces and pointing.
- One build at a time. Two versions are compared on different nights, alternating which goes first.

## What to log (PLAYTESTS.md, right after)

```markdown
## 2026-09-29 · Night Flight (owls) · build 4b1c2 · Juneau (5), little sister (2), Jon
- Started without help? yes/no. Where did they get stuck?
- Laughed at: … Pointed at the TV at: … Talked to each other about: …
- Tapped things that aren't buttons: … Ignored: …
- Little sister did: …
- Asked to play again? now / tomorrow / no. (Check the next day; this is Replay pull.)
- Grown-up friction: …
- One change that would matter most: …
```

## Turning it into work

- Each log line that names a problem becomes a FINDINGS entry tagged `[kid]` and a backlog item in
  the team's STATUS.
- A playtest verdict overrides the critic. A critic 8 with a kid who wandered off is not an 8.
- Kid feedback about *content* (wants dinosaurs, wants their name) goes to IDEAS.md as a signal for
  the catalog.
